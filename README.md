#  Monetary Transmission Terminal (MTT)
### Interactive Econometric Interface for Indian Banking Analysis

**Project Overview:**
This terminal simulates the **Asymmetric Impact of Monetary Policy Shocks** on Indian Scheduled Commercial Banks. Leveraging a **Panel Quantile Regression** framework, it demonstrates how repo rate adjustments propagate through different bank tiers (Public vs. Private) and sectors.

**Key Features:**
- **Dynamic Simulation:** Real-time credit growth forecasting based on RBI policy rate shifts.
- **Asymmetric Modeling:** Accounts for the non-linear response of banks to contractionary vs. expansionary shocks.
- **Sectoral Analysis:** Visualizing the divergent impact on Consumer vs. Industrial lending.

**Technical Stack:**
- **Language:** Python 3.12
- **UI Framework:** Streamlit (Custom CSS/Bootstrap)
- **Visualization:** Plotly Express & Graph Objects (High-Fidelity)
- **Data Source:** Synthesized based on RBI DBIE Reporting Frameworks.

**Research Context:**
Developed to bridge the gap between complex econometric modeling and policy-level communication, focusing on the 'Bank Lending Channel' of transmission in the Indian context.
